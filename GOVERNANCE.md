# Governance

LightPHI uses a maintainer-led model. Federico Forti is the initial maintainer
and release owner. Maintainers review changes, decide release scope, and may
appoint or remove other maintainers based on sustained project work.

Technical decisions happen in public issues and pull requests. The maintainer
seeks agreement from affected contributors. When agreement is not reached, the
release owner records the decision and its reasons in the issue.

One approving maintainer is required for ordinary changes. Stable ABI changes,
licence changes, and new production dependencies require the release owner's
approval. A contributor must not approve their own change.

Security reports follow `SECURITY.md` and remain private until a coordinated
disclosure is ready. Project conduct must be professional, respectful, and
focused on the technical work. Harassment, discrimination, and disclosure of
private information are not accepted. Maintainers may moderate or reject
participation that breaks these rules.
